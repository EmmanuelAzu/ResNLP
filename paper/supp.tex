\subsection*{S4. Anchors for the directional test}
Anchors are the 15 top-PMI neighbours of each seed pair in held-out text (Isigidimi 1876-83 + Imvo 1889-94), frozen before testing. Religious (15 words; seeds \emph{tixo}, \emph{kristu}): \emph{sikelela}, \emph{sindisa}, \emph{ukumkanikazi}, \emph{inkosazana}, \emph{buka}, \emph{oyingcwele}, \emph{lilizwi}, \emph{msindisi}, \emph{nelizwi}, \emph{kwelizwi}, \emph{wanga}, \emph{lika}, \emph{yesu}, \emph{izono}, \emph{yehova}. Political (15 words; seeds \emph{palamente}, \emph{voti}): \emph{emqulwini}, \emph{wonyulo}, \emph{lonyulo}, \emph{elugqatsweni}, \emph{eziqingata}, \emph{lokunyula}, \emph{kwesase}, \emph{lokuvota}, \emph{endlu}, \emph{kwamagama}, \emph{ukumela}, \emph{izayo}, \emph{ngalomteto}, \emph{nawupina}, \emph{kwabase}. Seed words cannot themselves be tested (Fig.~1, right). Political anchors include \emph{endlu} (`in the house'), which may favour the neutral control \emph{house}, and \emph{ngalomteto}, which contains the \emph{law} stem. Glosses are unverified: two religious anchors (\emph{ukumkanikazi}, \emph{inkosazana}) may reflect royal rather than religious register, and several anchors on both sides (\emph{lika}, \emph{buka}; \emph{nawupina}, \emph{kwabase}, \emph{izayo}) may be grammatical rather than topical, consistent with narrow-window PMI capturing grammar. All await speaker review.
